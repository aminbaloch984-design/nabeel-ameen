About AutoDrive PK

We are building a calmer way to buy and sell cars in Pakistan — documented history, honest condition notes and real people on the other end of the conversation.

Our story

The used car market deserved better

Non-genuine paintwork, altered auction sheets and rolled-back odometers are still routine in too many deals. AutoDrive PK exists to make the paperwork as visible as the paint.

Every listing follows the same structure: verified specifications, a condition summary, a clear PKR price with Lacs / Crore conversion, and a direct line to the seller. Optional inspections add paint-depth readings and a mechanical checklist on top.

How it works

From first search to signed transfer

A simple, documented path for both sides of the deal.

1 · Search with real filters

Filter by city, budget, year, mileage and transmission. Prices always show both full PKR and Lacs / Crore.

2 · Verify the dossier

Read the condition summary, ownership history and inspection sheet. Ask for the smart card and token tax proof.

3 · Transfer with proof

Meet in daylight, use bank transfers, and complete biometric verification before handing over possession.

Buyer safety

Four rules that prevent most losses

Sample guidance
Meet in daylightChoose a busy public location or an inspection hub. Avoid night handovers.
Verify excise recordsCheck token tax and registration status online before paying anything.
Pay by bank onlyNever hand cash to a third party or pay before seeing the vehicle.
Complete biometric transferPossession should change hands only after the transfer is stamped.

Questions

Frequently asked

Short answers to the things buyers and sellers ask most often.

Ask on WhatsApp
Are the listings real cars I can buy?

No — this is a design preview. Every listing on the site is sample data used to show the layout. Replace assets/js/data.js with real inventory before launch.

How does the WhatsApp contact work?

The button opens a pre-filled chat with a configurable business number. No phone number is exposed publicly, and sellers can choose to keep it that way.

What does the 210-point inspection include?

Engine and transmission behaviour, paint-depth readings, chassis and underbody condition, brakes, tyres, electricals and documentation checks, summarised in a shareable report.

Can I finance a car through AutoDrive PK?

The calculator is an estimate only. Actual approval, tenure and markup depend on the bank or Islamic financing partner and your credit profile.

How do I list my car?

Use the five-step form on the Sell Your Car page. In this demo, submissions are saved as a draft in your browser's local storage — publishing needs a backend.

Do you charge commission on private ads?

Private ads are free in the intended model. Paid visibility packages and optional inspections are separate add-ons.
