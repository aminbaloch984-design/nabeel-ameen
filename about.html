Ask a question
Are the laptops brand new and sealed?

Yes. Every laptop listed on LaptopHub is brand new unless the listing explicitly says otherwise. You receive a sealed box with an official warranty card and a matching serial number.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

COD is available in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi for orders up to PKR 5,00,000. Above that, we use bank transfer or card payment with order verification.

What does the warranty cover?

The standard 1-year manufacturer warranty covers hardware faults. Accidental damage and liquid damage are not covered unless you add the optional protection plan at checkout.

Can I return a laptop if I change my mind?

Unopened boxes can be returned within 7 days for a full refund. Opened units are covered by the manufacturer warranty and our diagnostic service, but not by change-of-mind returns.

Do you offer installment plans?

Yes — 0% installment plans for 3, 6 and 12 months are available through partner banks and wallet providers, subject to approval. Our team can pre-check your eligibility on WhatsApp.

Do you sell to companies and schools?

We do. Bulk orders of 10+ units get dedicated account management, consolidated invoicing and optional imaging before dispatch. Contact us with your requirement.

Privacy at a glance

We collect only the details needed to fulfil your order — name, phone, delivery address and payment confirmation. We never sell customer data, and marketing emails are opt-in with a one-click unsubscribe. This demo storefront stores your cart locally in your own browser; nothing is sent to a server.
