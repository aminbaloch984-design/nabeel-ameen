NEW AI

Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

NEW AI is a personal AI assistant built by Nabeel Ameen. It runs as a web app and, when installed from a store, as a lightweight app around the same web experience. This page explains exactly what happens to your data.

Short version: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no selling of data. Your chats are stored on your own device. A message is sent to the AI provider only at the moment you ask for an answer.

What stays on your device

Conversations, memories, saved tasks, permission choices and settings are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us and are not synced between devices.

You can export them as a JSON file, or delete them at any time from Settings → Local data. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data also removes them permanently.

What is sent when you send a message

When you ask a question, the text of your message (plus the recent conversation context and any memories you have enabled) is sent to our small proxy server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer. The proxy does not store the content of your messages.

  • The provider processes the text to produce a reply. Its own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.
  • We do not use your messages to train models.
  • Requests are encrypted in transit with HTTPS.

Do not send passwords, private keys, financial details or other secrets you would not want a third-party AI provider to process.

Voice

Speech-to-text uses the speech recognition built into your browser or operating system, which may send short audio to that vendor (for example Google) to transcribe it. Spoken replies are generated on your device by the system text-to-speech engine. We never receive your microphone audio.

Server logs

Our proxy server and the network in front of it may record basic technical information such as the time of a request, the response status and an IP address. This is used only to keep the service running and to stop abuse, and is not linked to the content of your conversations.

Cookies and analytics

NEW AI does not set advertising cookies and does not include third-party analytics or tracking scripts. The only storage used is the local storage described above, plus the app's offline cache, which keeps the interface files on your device.

Advertising

The current version shows no ads. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before they appear, and any ad partner (for example Google AdMob) will be named here together with the choices available to you.

Children

NEW AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn memories off, or delete individual memories, from the Memory page.
  • Export or erase everything from Settings → Local data.
  • Use the app without voice by leaving the microphone off.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes too. Significant changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer through the project's issue tracker.
